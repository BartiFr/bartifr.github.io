Orbiday

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Wersja polska

Orbiday works on your phone. It needs no account and contains no analytics. Your tasks, finances, workouts and other entries stay on your phone, and we sell nothing. The app shows Google AdMob ads, at most two a day — see “Ads” below.

Your data. Tasks, meals, recipes, finances, workouts, body measurements, habits, attachments and voice notes are stored only in the app's storage on this phone. Exports and backups you create are saved to the Downloads folder on your phone — what happens to them is up to you. If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep an encrypted copy of the app's database and settings in your Google account; photos, receipts and recordings are not part of that copy.

Location. If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location only to show the weather. Coordinates rounded to about one kilometre are sent to OpenWeather (OpenWeather Ltd.), which returns the weather for that place. The app keeps no location history. Without this permission you can pick a town by hand or do without the weather.

Microphone. It records only when you record a voice note for a task yourself. The recording stays on your phone.

Photos and camera. Photos of recipes and receipts are recognised on your phone by Google ML Kit and are not sent anywhere. Google libraries (ML Kit, the document scanner) may send Google anonymous technical data about how they work, such as the device model and processing time, not linked to you.

Content from the internet. Dish photos are downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, and exercise technique videos are played by YouTube (Google). As with any internet connection, these services see your phone's IP address; YouTube videos are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads. At the end of the Day screen, below your tasks, the app shows a card with a Google AdMob ad (Google Ireland Limited) — at most two ads a day, each shown for an hour. The first day after installing is ad-free. To choose and measure ads, Google collects your IP address (from which an approximate location follows), the device's advertising ID, the App Set ID, data about interactions with the app and the ad, and diagnostic data, sent over an encrypted connection. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app first asks for your consent with a Google form; without consent, ads are not tailored to you. You can change your decision in Settings → Ads, and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Notifications and alarms are scheduled and shown locally on your phone.

Children. The app is not intended for children under 13.

Deleting data. Uninstalling the app or using “Clear data” in your phone's settings deletes all app data. Files saved in the Downloads folder are yours to delete.

Contact. Send privacy questions to the developer e-mail address shown on Orbiday's Google Play page.
